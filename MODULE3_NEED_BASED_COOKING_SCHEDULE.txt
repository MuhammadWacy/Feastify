Feastify - Module 3 / Member 2 / Feature 2
Listing Need-Based Cooking Schedule

CUSTOMER FLOW
1. Customer opens Navbar -> Post a Need.
2. Customer posts event name/date, dish, preparation details, servings, price per serving, delivery location, contact number and optional notes.
3. The post is stored in MongoDB as a NeedPost with status=open.
4. Customer can see their own posts and cancel an open post.
5. When a caterer confirms the need, it becomes an approved ServiceRequest automatically.
6. Customer opens Orders / Live Order Tracking and pays exactly like a normal approved order.
7. Existing payment, preparing/on-the-way progress, delivery verification, proof photo and OneSignal notification continue to work.

SELLER FLOW
1. Seller Home is now also the Customer Need Feed.
2. Open needs are sorted by eventDate ascending, so earlier requirements appear first.
3. Clicking a card opens a dedicated detail page.
4. Seller confirms the need.
5. Backend atomically claims the NeedPost, creates an approved need_based ServiceRequest, and links the two.
6. The accepted order appears on Seller Dashboard with the customer's custom instructions and delivery/contact details.
