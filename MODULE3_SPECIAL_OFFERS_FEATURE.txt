FEASTIFY - MODULE 3 / MEMBER 3 / FEATURE 1
SPECIAL OFFERS

SELLER FLOW
- Seller navbar -> Special Offers.
- Seller must already have a published catering listing.
- Seller can create, edit, pause/reactivate, and delete a special offer.
- Offer fields: name, details, price per serving, minimum servings, maximum servings, validity date, and optional image.
- Images are uploaded to Cloudinary and their URL/public ID are stored in MongoDB.

CUSTOMER FLOW
- Active, unexpired offers from published caterers appear in the Special Offers section of Customer Home.
- Customer opens an offer, selects servings within the seller's range and an event/delivery date within the validity period.
- Existing caterer operating-day restrictions are also respected.
- The offer is added to the existing Feastify cart.
- Request Service creates the normal pending ServiceRequest for that seller.
- Seller can approve/reject it as usual.
- If approved, customer can pay, seller can mark Preparing / On the Way, and Delivery Verification works exactly as for regular orders.

SELLER API
GET    /api/special-offers/mine
POST   /api/special-offers
PUT    /api/special-offers/:id
DELETE /api/special-offers/:id

CUSTOMER CATALOG
GET /api/catalog/offers

ORDER INTEGRATION
POST /api/service-requests
- specialOfferId is included for special-offer bookings.
- Backend re-checks that the offer exists, is active, has not expired, the event date is within validity, serving quantity is within the offer range, and the price/title/image are taken from MongoDB rather than trusting the browser.
