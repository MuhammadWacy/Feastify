FEASTIFY MODULE 2 - FAVORITES

Customer flow:
1. Open Customer Home.
2. Click the heart on any caterer card to add/remove it from Favorites.
3. Open Favorites from the customer navbar.
4. Saved caterers appear as cards.
5. Click View Caterer Profile to open the customer-facing caterer profile.
6. The profile shows public business information and current available menu listings.
7. A Customer Reviews section is reserved on the profile for the future review feature.

Persistence:
- Favorites are saved in MongoDB on the customer User document as references to Catering listings.
- Unpublished caterers are not returned in the Favorites page.

New backend API routes:
GET    /api/favorites
POST   /api/favorites/:cateringId
DELETE /api/favorites/:cateringId
GET    /api/catalog/caterings/:id/profile

No new npm packages or environment variables are required.
