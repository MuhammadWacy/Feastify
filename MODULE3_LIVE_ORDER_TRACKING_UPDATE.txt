FEASTIFY - MODULE 3 / MEMBER 2 / FEATURE 1
LIVE ORDER TRACKING PROGRESS UPDATE

Added statuses:
- Preparing Food
- On the Way / Being Delivered

Behavior:
- Existing Pending, Approved, Rejected, Payment and Delivered flows are unchanged.
- Seller can update live progress only for approved + paid + not-yet-delivered orders.
- If seller never selects a live progress status, customer sees the old behavior exactly as before.
- Customer sees Preparing Food only after seller marks it.
- Customer sees On the Way only after seller marks it.
- Seller can clear a live update if it was selected accidentally.
- Delivered remains controlled by the existing Delivery Verification feature with proof image and OneSignal notification.

- backend/models/ServiceRequest.js
  orderProgressStatus: "", "preparing", "on_the_way"
  orderProgressUpdatedAt
- backend/controllers/serviceRequestController.js

- backend/routes/serviceRequestRoutes.js
