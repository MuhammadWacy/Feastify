MODULE 3 - MEMBER 4 FEATURE 1
SERVICE BOOKING HISTORY

PURPOSE
- Gives customers and caterers a separate permanent history of completed catering services.
- Only orders whose deliveryStatus is "delivered" appear.
- Orders enter this history only after the existing seller Delivery Verification workflow succeeds.

CUSTOMER
Navbar -> Booking History
Route: /customer/history
Shows:
- Caterer name/email
- Order type (regular, negotiated, need-based, special offer)
- Event date and delivery date/time
- Amount paid and payment details
- Total servings
- Each ordered dish and its original menu/offer image when available
- Price per serving and item subtotal
- Need-based custom details when applicable
- Delivery verification photo

SELLER
Navbar -> Booking History
Route: /seller/history
Shows the same completed service record from the caterer's perspective, including the customer identity and all order details.

BACKEND
GET /api/service-requests/history/customer
GET /api/service-requests/history/seller
Both endpoints are protected and role-checked.
Both query ServiceRequest with deliveryStatus: "delivered" and sort newest delivery first.

DATABASE
No duplicate history collection is required. ServiceRequest already contains the authoritative completed-order data, including menu item image URLs and delivery proof image URL. The history page reads those delivered ServiceRequest documents directly.
