FEASTIFY - MODULE 3 / MEMBER 4 / FEATURE 2
VERIFIED POST-DELIVERY REVIEWS + CATERER REPLIES

Customer flow
-------------
1. A Post Review button appears only after seller Delivery Verification marks an order delivered.
2. Customer selects a 1-5 rating, writes a detailed review, and may upload up to 5 images.
3. One review is allowed per delivered ServiceRequest.
4. Review images are stored in Cloudinary.
5. The review becomes visible on the customer-facing caterer profile.

Public review privacy
---------------------
The public caterer profile deliberately does not expose payment amount, payment reference,
customer email/phone/address, event date/location, or other sensitive booking fields.
It shows only a shortened reviewer name, rating, review text/images, delivery date,
total servings, dish names/images/details, and the caterer's public reply.

Caterer flow
------------
Seller navbar -> Reviews
- View verified reviews filed against the seller.
- Reply to each review separately.
- Update an existing reply.
- Replies become visible underneath that review on the customer-facing caterer profile.

Notes
-----
- Review eligibility is re-validated on the backend using ServiceRequest.deliveryStatus and deliveredAt.
- The backend ignores client-supplied order metadata and builds the public review snapshot from MongoDB.
- Caterer rating is recalculated from verified reviews after each new review.
- Existing complaint, booking history, delivery verification, payment, negotiation, special offer,
  need-based order, FAQ, favorites, search, weather, and AI Assistant flows are preserved.
